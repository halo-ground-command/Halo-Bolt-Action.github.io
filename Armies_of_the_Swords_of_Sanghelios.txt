Armies of the Swords_of_Sangheliosv1.1

Use the Covenant army list, replacing the Covenant Army Special Rules with the rules below. 
Army Special Rules
Sangheili Command 
The Swords of Sanghelios fight under the direct leadership of Sangheili warriors, whose presence keeps allied troops focused and steady in battle.
* Effect: Swords of Sanghelios Infantry and Artillery units that are within 6" of at least one friendly Elite unit and are not within an Officer’s morale bubble gain a +1 Morale bonus.
Disciplined Under Fire 
Sangheili-led veteran warriors are trained to endure bombardment, suppressive fire, and sudden battlefield disruption without breaking formation. 
* Effect: Each time a Veteran Swords of Sanghelios unit would gain 2 or more Pin markers from HE or another source, reduce the number of Pin markers gained by 1. 








Army Restrictions
* A Swords of Sanghelios army cannot include Brutes.
* Units with For The Great Journey! may still be upgraded as Grunts/Jackals or Elites as normal.
Rite of Challenge 
Sangheili warriors prize honourable close combat, but only those who bear the proper weapons can truly answer such a challenge. 
* Effect: When an Elite Infantry unit that includes at least one model with a Melee Weapon is given a Run order to assault an enemy unit, it counts as having the Fieldcraft special rule for that action. 
War-Chants of Sanghelios 
The Swords of Sanghelios advance beneath ancient battle-chants, open-channel challenges, and rolling war-cries.  
* Effect: At the start of every Game Turn, roll a D6 for each enemy Infantry and Artillery unit on the table that has no Pin markers. On a 5+, that unit immediately receives 1 Pin marker. Do not roll for units embarked inside transports.
