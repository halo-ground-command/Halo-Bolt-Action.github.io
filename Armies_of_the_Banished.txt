Armies of the Banishedv3.6

Army Special Rules
Brotherhood of Chaos
The Banished ranks are composed of fierce but unruly factions, each warband eager to strike first and claim the spoils before its rivals.
* Effect: Once per turn, when a Banished Infantry unit enters the battlefield from Reserve or Outflanking, it may enter with a Run order and still fire its Small Arms after moving. If it does so, those weapons suffer a -1 to hit penalty, even if they have Assault. 
Trophy Takers 
Banished Brutes tear armour, trophies, and wargear from the defeated, wearing each victory as proof of dominance. 
* Effect: The first time a Banished Brute Infantry unit wins a Close Quarters combat, it gains Trophy Plate Harness for the rest of the battle. The first time any friendly unit that began the battle with Trophy Plate Harness wins a Close Quarters combat, improve its Trophy Plate Harness save to 5+ in Close Quarters for the rest of the battle. 


Blood Price
Banished leaders do not inspire loyalty—they enforce obedience through fear and brutality.
* Effect: When a friendly Banished Infantry or Artillery unit fails an Order test while within 6″ of a friendly Banished Officer or Bloodhandler (including FUBAR), you may choose to remove one model from the unit and then re-roll the Order test. The second result stands as normal. This rule only applies to Infantry or Artillery units of three or more models.
Atriox’s Law
Though prone to bloodlust, Banished warriors will restrain themselves in the presence of a commanding officer.
* Effect: Units with the Berserker special rule can ignore the compulsory Run behavior caused by failing an Order test while they are within the morale bubble of an Officer. The suppression ends immediately if the unit exits the bubble or if the officer is removed from play.








FORGED IN CHAINS
This rule allows you to customize Banished infantry and artillery units to represent the different warrior castes within the Banished hierarchy. Each eligible unit may be upgraded individually — you can freely mix and match Elite, Brute, and other squad types within the same force.
Units whose profiles include “FORGED IN CHAINS” in their Special Rules section may benefit from this customization.
When selected, apply all associated rules and point costs to every model in the squad. Mixed designations are not allowed within the same unit.

Banished Armoury
Scrap Armour Screens
Banished armoured vehicles with Damage Value 7+ or higher may take Scrap Armour Screens for +10pts. This upgrade may not be taken by Walkers or Aircraft.
Weapons with Shaped Charge do not gain +1 Penetration bonus for hitting this vehicle in the side arc.
Trophy Plate Harness
Veteran Banished Brute Infantry units may take Trophy Plate Harnesses for +1pt per model.
Models with Trophy Plate Harnesses have a 6+ cover save in Close Quarters.
Melee Weapon Upgrades
NCO/Officer models may upgrade a Melee Weapon into a special melee weapon. The model must have a Melee Weapon. Teams and Artillery units cannot take these upgrades. A model that takes one of these upgrades keeps the +1 melee attack from having a Melee Weapon.
Plasma Sword
An Elite NCO or Officer with a Melee Weapon may take a Plasma Sword using one of the following options:
* If the model has no ranged weapon, it may take a Plasma Sword for +6pts.
* If the model is armed with a Plasma Rifle or Needler, it may add a Plasma Sword for +5pts.
If the unit already has the Tough Fighters special rule, reduce the cost of this upgrade by 1pt.
A model with a Plasma Sword gains Tough Fighters. When this model fights in close quarters, it may use the Plasma Sword. If it does, reduce this model’s number of total attacks by 1. Attacks made with the Plasma Sword gain +1 to wound against Infantry and Artillery units. If these attacks cause at least one casualty, you may choose an enemy NCO, Officer, or unit leader in the target unit as one of the casualties caused by this model.
Gravity Hammer
A Brute NCO or Officer with a Melee Weapon may take a Gravity Hammer using one of the following options:
* If the model has a Spiker or Plasma Rifle, it may replace that ranged weapon with a Gravity Hammer for no additional cost.
* If the model has no ranged weapon, it may take a Gravity Hammer for +8pts.
If the unit already has the Tough Fighters special rule, reduce the points cost of this upgrade by 1pt.
A model with a Gravity Hammer gains Tough Fighters. In addition, while this unit is fighting in close quarters, the Gravity Hammer wielder gains +1 extra melee attack for every full 3 enemy models involved in the fight. If a unit containing a Gravity Hammer is assaulted in open ground while it is not Down, it counts as having Defensive Position.
Brute Grenadiers 
A Banished Grenade Launcher Team selected as Brutes may be upgraded to Brute Grenadiers for +20pts. Double the number of shots made by this unit’s weapons. In addition, if this unit is destroyed in Close Quarters, the enemy unit involved immediately suffers one HE (1") hit.
