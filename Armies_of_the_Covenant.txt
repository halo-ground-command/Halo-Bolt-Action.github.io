Armies of the Covenant v3.4

Army Special Rules
Caste System
The Covenant draws its strength from the unique abilities of its member species, each excelling in different aspects of warfare.
* Effect: When creating a Covenant army, you may select one bonus from the following list:
   * Commanding Presence: Covenant Infantry and artillery units that are within 6″ of at least one Elite unit and not within an officer’s morale bubble gain a +1 Morale bonus.
   * Cannon Fodder: Your army gets a free basic inexperienced Grunt squad unit.
   * Wall of Deflection: Friendly units with Shield Wall or Resilient provide hard cover instead of soft cover to other friendly units when the line of fire passes through them.
   * Fury Unleashed: Covenant infantry units that fail their Run/Assault order test when within 9" of a Brute unit may re-roll the failed order test.
Zealotry
Covenant troops are fanatically devoted to their cause, fighting without fear of death.
* Effect: Regular or Veteran Covenant units ignore the Morale penalty from 1 Pin when taking Advance or Run order tests, if they are not benefiting from a friendly morale bonus bubble.
Orbital Superiority 
The Covenant dominate the skies above the battlefield, directing precision plasma bombardments and strikes from orbit and high altitude. 
* Once per game, when a friendly Covenant Forward Observer resolves an Artillery Barrage or Air Strike, you may roll two D6 instead of one on the Artillery Barrage Table or Air Strike Table and choose which result to apply. 
Faithful When Supervised
Covenant troops fight bravely—so long as a superior is watching.
* Effect: Covenant Infantry and Artillery units with Shirkers lose that rule while they are positioned within the morale bubble of a friendly Covenant Officer. If they exit the bubble or the Officer is lost, they regain Shirkers until within range of another valid Officer. If a unit exits the Officer’s morale bubble as part of a Snap to Action activation, it cannot regain Shirkers until the Officer and all units activated through that Snap have completed their orders.
FOR THE GREAT JOURNEY!
This rule allows you to customize Covenant infantry and artillery units to represent the different warrior castes within the Covenant hierarchy. Each eligible unit may be upgraded individually — you can freely mix and match Elite, Brute, and other squad types within the same force.
Units whose profiles include “FOR THE GREAT JOURNEY!” in their Special Rules section may benefit from this customization.
When selected, apply all associated rules and point costs to every model in the squad. Mixed designations are not allowed within the same unit.
GRUNTS/JACKALS
Default. No Bonuses.
BRUTES
Must be taken as Regular or Veteran.
Gain the Stubborn, Strong, Fieldcraft and Berserker special rules.
Cost: +4pts per model.
Optional additional upgrades (Brute-only):
 – The squad may be given Energy Shields for +3pts per model. 
– The NCO/Officer may be given a Melee Weapon for +1pt (This model rolls 1 additional dice in close combat)
ELITES
Must be taken as Veteran.
Gain the Behind Enemy Lines, Fanatics, Energy Shields, Fast, Strong, Strikes First and VISR special rules.
Cost: +9pts per model.
Optional additional upgrades (Elite-only):
 – The squad may be given Stealth for +1pts per model. 
– The NCO/Officer may be given a Melee Weapon for +1pt (This model rolls 1 additional dice in close combat) 


























Covenant Armoury
Sacred Relic
One Veteran Elite model in your army may include a Sacred Relic for +15pts. The Sacred Relic cannot be carried by an Officer or NCO.
Friendly Covenant Infantry and Artillery units within 12" and line of sight of the Elite model with the Sacred Relic may re-roll failed Morale checks.
Plasma Capacitor Cells
Veteran Covenant Infantry units without Team Weapon may take Plasma Capacitor Cells for +1pt per model.
When weapons in this unit use Overheat, roll one D6 for each weapon that used Overheat after resolving the shooting attack. For each roll of 4+, remove one Pin marker generated by that Overheat.
Melee Weapon Upgrades
NCO/Officer models may upgrade a Melee Weapon into a special melee weapon. The model must have a Melee Weapon. Teams and Artillery units cannot take these upgrades. A model that takes one of these upgrades keeps the +1 melee attack from having a Melee Weapon.
Plasma Sword
An Elite NCO or Officer with a Melee Weapon may take a Plasma Sword using one of the following options:
* If the model has no ranged weapon, it may take a Plasma Sword for +6pts.
* If the model is armed with a Plasma Rifle or Needler, it may add a Plasma Sword for +5pts.
If the unit already has the Tough Fighters special rule, reduce the cost of this upgrade by 1pt.
A model with a Plasma Sword gains Tough Fighters. When this model fights in close quarters, it may use the Plasma Sword. If it does, reduce this model’s number of total attacks by 1. Attacks made with the Plasma Sword gain +1 to wound against Infantry and Artillery units. If these attacks cause at least one casualty, you may choose an enemy NCO, Officer, or unit leader in the target unit as one of the casualties caused by this model.
Gravity Hammer
A Brute NCO or Officer with a Melee Weapon may take a Gravity Hammer using one of the following options:
* If the model has a Spiker or Plasma Rifle, it may replace that ranged weapon with a Gravity Hammer for no additional cost.
* If the model has no ranged weapon, it may take a Gravity Hammer for +8pts.
If the unit already has the Tough Fighters special rule, reduce the points cost of this upgrade by 1pt.
A model with a Gravity Hammer gains Tough Fighters. In addition, while this unit is fighting in close quarters, the Gravity Hammer wielder gains +1 extra melee attack for every full 3 enemy models involved in the fight. If a unit containing a Gravity Hammer is assaulted in open ground while it is not Down, it counts as having Defensive Position.
