Armies of the Floodv3.3

Army Special Rules
Living Nightmare
All Flood embody terror and relentless aggression.
* Effect: All Flood Infantry and Artillery units have the Fanatics special rule, and automatically pass any Morale Checks.
Corrupted Arsenal
The Flood assimilate their enemies' equipment and vehicles, turning them against their creators.
* Effect: The Flood may include vehicles from other factions' army lists. These vehicles must always be inexperienced.
Predatory Ambush
The Flood can fall into an unnatural, corpse-like stillness, only to erupt in a sudden burst of violence when prey strays too close.
* Effect: When a Flood Infantry unit ends the turn on Ambush and chooses to attempt the normal end-of-turn Fire action, roll a D6 as usual. On a 4+, the unit may either fire normally or, instead of firing, declare an Assault against a visible enemy Infantry or Artillery unit within assault range. On a 3 or less, the unit does nothing and its Ambush die is returned to the bag as normal.
Hivemind Coordination
The Flood’s collective consciousness ensures cohesion even when individual leaders fall.
* Effect: Flood Infantry and Artillery units do not suffer the –1 morale penalty for losing their NCO.
Logic Plague
Flood presence corrupts and destabilizes synthetic minds, causing unpredictable malfunctions.
* Effect: Once per turn, just before activating a Flood unit, select one unactivated enemy Automaton or Composed Being within 6" of any friendly Flood unit and roll a D6:
   * 1-3: The enemy immediately receives 2 Pin markers.
   * 4-5: The enemy suffers –1 to hit for the rest of the turn.
   * 6: The enemy must take a morale check. If failed, roll on the FUBAR table; if passed, no effect.










Flood Armoury
Burrowing Tendrils
Flood Officer units that contain tendrils may take Burrowing Tendrils for +2pt per model. If taken, the unit gains the Infiltrator and Stealth special rules.
Additionally, once per army, when one unit with this rule is deployed fully within an eligible terrain piece using Infiltrator deployment, that terrain piece may become Infested Terrain for the rest of the game. This terrain piece cannot be a road, objective marker, bunker, impassable terrain, or a building/terrain piece containing an objective.
Infested Terrain: At the end of an enemy non-Flood Infantry or Artillery unit’s activation, if that unit moved across, through, or into contact with Infested Terrain during that activation, or has at least one model inside or touching Infested Terrain, that unit gains 1 Pin marker. A unit can only gain 1 Pin per turn from Infested Terrain. 
Patient Predators
The following units can take Patient Predators for +1pt per model:
- Officer
- Combat Form Squad
- Carrier Form Team
- Anti Tank Team
- Grenade Launcher Team
- Sniper Team
- Spawner Form
If a unit with this rule is deemed to be in cover from enemy shooting while it has an Ambush order, improve its Cover Save by +1. Remember rolls of 1 always fail.
Spore Cloud
Flood Infantry and Artillery units may take Spore Cloud for +1pt per model. 
When this unit is destroyed, place one 3" smoke marker anywhere within 6" of the last model removed. This smoke marker follows the normal Smoke rules, with the following exception:
When a unit with Flood Infection wins Close Quarters, it may roll one additional Flood Infection die if either unit in that combat is within 3" of a Spore Cloud marker.
Flood Burster
Infection Form Swarms in your army may take Flood Burster for +1pt per model. This represents Bursters erupting from below and releasing Infection Forms into the battlefield.
When this unit successfully enters from Reserve it may deploy via Flood Burster instead of entering from a table edge. Place the unit legally anywhere in open ground more than 9" from all enemy units, then give it a Down order. It cannot perform more actions that turn. If no legal position is available, the unit fails to enter from Reserve and remains off-table.
Only one unit may deploy by eruption each turn, even if several units in your army have Flood Burster.
Volatile Biomass
A Carrier Form Team may take Volatile Biomass for +5pts. If taken, increase the HE value of Explosive Biomass from HE (1") to HE (2").
